# 🍲 Hearth & Spice

> **Less Stress. More Soul.**  
> *Mood-curated food delivery and authentic culinary heritage, powered by Google AI.*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Hearth & Spice** redefines meal discovery by matching food suggestions directly to your emotional state—eliminating endless scrolling and decision fatigue. Whether you need comfort food after a long day or vibrant flavors to celebrate, Hearth & Spice connects you to curated dishes and step-by-step recipes in minutes.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🧠 Mood-Based Curation:** Intelligent meal recommendations tailored to your current energy, craving, or mood.
- **📖 Step-by-Step Heritage Recipes:** Interactive cooking timelines featuring detailed chef techniques, physical cues, and tips for classic dishes.
- **🛡️ Smart Resilience & Quota Protection:** Integrated local fallback library (*SoulFood Kitchen*) ensuring zero downtime and instant recipe access even during API quota limits.
- **🔐 Modern Authentication:** Streamlined Email signup/login with seamless optional Google Sign-In via Firebase.
- **⚡ Real-Time Backend:** Powered by Firestore with strict security rules, action-based order updates, and automatic connection cleanup.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend & AI:** Google AI Studio (Gemini 3.8 Flash), React, TypeScript
- **Backend & Database:** Node.js, Express, Firebase Firestore, Firebase Auth
- **Styling:** Modern, responsive UI optimized for desktop and mobile
